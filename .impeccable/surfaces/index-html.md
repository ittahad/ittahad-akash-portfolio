# Surface brief — index.html (single-page portfolio), REDESIGN 2026-11

## Scope
Single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Persuade (hiring managers screening senior backend candidates). Replaces the awmedia-derived black monochrome world; user pinned "authentic, human, cool" and chose "calm workshop journal", voice + hand-made details, no photo.

## Audience, job, action, proof
Recruiters/eng leaders; decide in one viewport; act = email. Proof = real roles, 9 projects, 6 articles, 8 repos already in the markup. No invented claims, testimonials, or imagery.

## Direction contract
THESIS. The page is Akash's engineering logbook: a person's working notes, not an agency stage. It refuses the dark-monochrome spectacle and the generic hero+cards template; trust comes from first-person voice, handwriting, and marks made by a hand.
OWN-WORLD. Dot-grid notebook paper in pale pistachio (#e7edd8) with blue-black ink (#15203a), ballpoint pen blue (#1f43d6) for annotations and links, marker-yellow highlight (#f7e35c), margin pink (#e0476e) for ticks and the "now" sticker. Night theme: ink-navy paper, chalk-cream ink. Open-source section is drenched in pen blue. Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display/body), Shantell Sans (handwriting marginalia), Geist Mono (dates only).
STORY. Visitor meets a person ("hi, I'm Akash"), reads one taped field-notes card (role, scale, countries, stack), and sees the receipts as dated entries, an index of projects, clippings of writing, and repos. Believes: senior, shipped at scale, writes it down. Does: email.
FIRST VIEWPORT. Typed role line, giant first-person headline across the full width with a marker-highlighted ".NET systems", lede with a hand-circled "four countries", primary Email button beside copy-email, and a slightly tilted taped field-notes card pulled up under the headline on the right. Dot-grid ground, no split hero.
FORM. Candidate: workshop logbook (user-pinned; roll skipped per pinned-direction rule). Seed key: none (pinned).
FINISH. unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
Marker highlights and pen strokes draw themselves as you scroll, like someone annotating the page while you read.
